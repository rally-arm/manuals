1

Overview

TT-Raid is a trip meter for rally raids that measures distance from GPS speed. Its two trips, TOTAL and PART, can be operated quickly with buttons and levers.

FeatureDescription
TripsTwo trips: TOTAL (upper row) and PART (lower row). You can correct them in 10 m steps or enter a distance directly. Both are kept when the power is off.
Trip selectionA lever selects whether TOTAL only, PART only, or both advance.
Upper displaySwitches between TOTAL, clock, heading, speed and stopwatch.
ClockGPS time plus your time-zone offset.
StopwatchTimes a stage, starting with a countdown to the next full minute.
Driver displayA separate display for the driver showing speed and PART.

Some display examples in this manual are live. The clock follows the time on your computer or phone, and the trips and speed change as if driving at 36 km/h (10 m per second).

Using the buttons and levers

A short press acts when you release the button. A long press acts once you have held it for 1.6 seconds.

A long press takes effect before you let go, so release the button when the display changes. Holding ▼10 or ▲10 for 0.8 seconds makes the value change continuously.

4

Upper display

Press FUNC briefly to cycle the upper row through five displays. The lower row always shows PART.

After 5 the display returns to 1. Heading is the angle measured clockwise from north (0°).

10 m corrections (▼10 / ▲10)

▲10 adds 10 m and ▼10 subtracts 10 m. Hold for 0.8 seconds to change continuously. Choose which trips are corrected with “±10 target” in the settings (default: both TOTAL and PART). A trip below 10 m is not reduced further.

Entering a distance

  1. Long press TOTAL for TOTAL, or PART for PART, to open the entry screen. Five digits (100 km to 10 m) can be entered.
  2. Move the blinking digit left or right with ▼10 / ▲10.
  3. TOTAL increases the digit and PART decreases it.
  4. Long press the button you started with (for TOTAL, TOTAL) to confirm.
6

Stopwatch (stage)

With the stopwatch on the upper row, press CALIB to start a countdown to 00 seconds of the next minute. Times are shown to the second.

  1. Start the countdown: in standby, press CALIB briefly. TOTAL and PART are reset to 0.
  2. Start: the stage starts automatically when the countdown reaches 0. Pressing CALIB during the countdown starts it immediately.
  3. Finish: while running, press CALIB briefly. The time is fixed and blinks, and PART is reset to 0.
  4. Back to standby: after the finish, press CALIB briefly.

Pressing FUNC during the countdown cancels it and returns to standby. If you switch the upper row to another display while running, timing continues. The stopwatch uses GPS time, so wait until the GPS LED is on before using it.

7

GPS and time

Distance, speed, heading and time all come from GPS. When the green GPS LED is on, GPS is being received.

The time shown is GPS time (UTC) plus your time-zone offset. The offset is +9 for Japan and +7 for Thailand, and can be changed in the settings (chapter 9).

8

Driver display

A separate display for the driver. It always shows speed and PART, whatever the main unit is showing.

RowShows
Upper (3 digits, 0.4 in)Current speed (1 km/h steps)
Lower (5 digits, 0.56 in)PART (0.00–999.99 km, 10 m steps)

Speed and PART use the same calibration factor as the main unit.

9

Settings

Long press FUNC to open the settings. Each short press of FUNC moves on: brightness, time-zone offset, ±10 target, then exit.

Change values with TOTAL (+) and PART (−). Changes are saved immediately. A short press of CALIB leaves the settings at any point.

10

Calibration

The calibration factor matches the displayed distance to the real distance. The display shows “GPS distance ÷ factor”, so increase the factor if the display reads too long.

For example, if an official 1.000 km reads 1.020 km, the factor is 1.0200.

  1. Long press CALIB to open the calibration screen.
  2. Move the blinking digit left or right with ▼10 / ▲10.
  3. TOTAL increases and PART decreases the digit (0.0000–2.9999). Changes are saved immediately.
  4. Long press CALIB again to return to the normal display.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
The GPS LED keeps blinkingGPS is not being received. Wait for a while in a place with a clear view of the sky.
The time is off by several hoursCheck the time-zone offset. Japan is 9, Thailand is 7.
Distance does not increaseCheck that the direction lever is not in the center (stop), that the trip select lever is not holding the trip, and that the GPS LED is on.
Only one trip increasesSet the trip select lever to the center (T&P).
▼10 / ▲10 changes only one tripCheck “±10 target” in the settings.
Distance does not match the official distanceAdjust the calibration factor, or enter the distance directly.
Pressing CALIB does not start a stageSwitch the upper row to the stopwatch first.

Settings kept when the power is off

TOTAL, PART, the calibration factor, brightness, time-zone offset and ±10 target are saved. The stopwatch state is lost.

Holding CALIB while switching on shows the software version on the lower row for 2 seconds.
