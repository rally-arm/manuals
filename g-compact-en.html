CHAPTER 01Safety precautions

Power supply
Power is supplied through the USB-C socket (5 V) at the end of the cable on the right side. When using the vehicle’s 12 V, use a USB power adapter. Never connect 12 V directly.
Operating while driving
All operation while driving should be done by the co-driver. The driver must concentrate on driving.
GPS start-up time
The first time you use the unit, or after it has been switched off for a long time, it may take several minutes before GPS measurement starts. Switch it on well before the event or drive.
GPS reception
Distance and speed are measured by GPS. Reception may not be possible under a roof or in tunnels. After switching on, check GPS reception (the display stops blinking) in an open area before use.
Mounting location
Do not use or store the unit where it can get wet or hot (e.g. in direct sunlight on the dashboard). Do not cover the light sensor.

CHAPTER 03Switching on

  1. Connect 5 V to the USB-C socket at the end of the power cable. The unit switches on automatically; there is no power switch.
  2. While searching for GPS, the display blinks.
  3. When GPS is received, the blinking stops and the correct time is shown.
What is kept
The last screen, trip distance, calibration, time offset, speed limit, km / miles and the trip display format are kept when the power is off.
At start-up the direction is forward (F) and the speed alert is OFF.

CHAPTER 04Switching screens

Each short press of the red button switches clock → trip → speed → clock …

CHAPTER 05Clock: time hold

  1. On the clock screen, a short press of the white button shows the time at the moment you pressed.
  2. It is held for 5 seconds. The separator dots blink while the time is held.
  3. After 5 seconds, the current time is shown again.

Useful for checking splits during practice runs. The remote button does the same.

CHAPTER 06Time offset

  1. On the clock screen, long press the red button to set the time offset. “Utc 9” means UTC+9 (Japan).
  2. Each short press of the white button adds 1 hour. After +14 it goes back to −12.
  3. A long press of the white button returns to +9.
  4. Press the red button to return to the clock. The setting is saved immediately.

CHAPTER 07Resetting the trip

  1. Switch to the trip screen. The leading “t” shows forward direction.
  2. A short press of the white button resets the trip to 0.
  3. Counting restarts from 0. The reset value is saved immediately.

CHAPTER 08Direction F / N / R

Each long press of the white button switches the direction F → N → R → F. It works on the clock, trip and speed screens.

DirectionActionDisplay
F (forward)Counts distance.Trip starts with “t”
N (stop)Does not count distance.Leading “n” blinks on the trip screen
R (reverse)Subtracts the distance driven (never below 0).Leading “r” blinks on the trip screen

In N or R, the dot on the left also blinks on the clock and speed screens. To return to F, long press twice more (N → R → F).

CHAPTER 09Distance calibration

When the displayed distance does not match the road book, adjust it with the calibration factor. Example: a 10.00 km section in the road book shows 10.200 km.

  1. On the trip screen, long press the red button to enter calibration (C).
  2. The white button adds 1 to the blinking digit; a short press of the red button moves to the next digit.
  3. Set 10.200 ÷ 10.00 = 1.020. A long press of the white button returns to 1.000.
  4. Long press the red button to save and return to the trip screen. The display now shows 10.000 km.
How to calculate
Calibration factor = G-Trip displayed distance ÷ road book distance

The factor can be set from 0.500 to 2.000. It also applies to the speed display.

CHAPTER 10Speed alert

  1. On the speed screen, a short press of the white button turns the speed alert ON. “A” blinks while it is ON.
  2. At or above the speed limit (60 by default) the whole display blinks fast. The alert also works on the clock and trip screens.
  3. Below the limit the blinking stops.
  4. To turn it OFF, press the white button briefly again on the speed screen.

After switching the power on again, the speed alert starts OFF.

CHAPTER 11Setting the speed limit

  1. On the speed screen, long press the red button to set the speed limit.
  2. Each short press of the white button adds 10. The range is 30–90; after 90 it goes back to 30.
  3. Long press the red button to save and return to the speed screen.

CHAPTER 12km / miles

  1. Switch the power on (connect USB-C) while holding the red button to toggle between km and miles.
  2. With miles, the mi lamp to the right of the display lights. Speed is shown in mph.
  3. Do the same to return to km.

CHAPTER 13Trip display format

There are two trip display formats. Switch the power on (connect USB-C) while holding the white button to toggle them. Do the same to return.

FormatDisplayMaximum

CHAPTER 15Automatic brightness

The small round window at the top left of the display is the light sensor. When it gets dark, the display dims automatically so it does not dazzle at night. When it gets bright again, the display returns to normal brightness.
