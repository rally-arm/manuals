CHAPTER 01Safety precautions

Power supply
Power the unit from either USB-C (5 V) or DC12V, never both. Supplying power from both at the same time may damage the unit.
Operating while driving
All operation while driving should be done by the co-driver. The driver must concentrate on driving; mount the driver display where it can be read with minimal eye movement.
GPS start-up time
The first time you use the unit, or after it has been switched off for a long time, it may take several minutes before GPS measurement starts. Switch it on well before the event or drive.
GPS reception
Distance and speed are measured by GPS. Reception may not be possible under a roof or in tunnels. After switching on, check GPS reception (green LED steady) in an open area before use.
Mounting location
Do not use or store the unit where it can get wet or hot (e.g. in direct sunlight on the dashboard).

CHAPTER 03Power and GPS

Using USB-C

  1. Connect 5 V to the USB-C port on the right side. The unit switches on automatically.
  2. Right after start-up the display shows “------”.
  3. While the green LED blinks, the unit is searching for GPS. When it stays on, GPS is received and the correct time is shown.

Using DC12V

  1. Connect DC12V (red) and GND (black) of the main harness on the rear to the vehicle.
  2. Press the lower side (|) of the red switch on the right side for ON, the upper side (○) for OFF.
  3. The switch is not used when powering from USB-C.
What is kept
Trip A, Trip B, calibration, time offset, brightness, the driver display distance format, speed limit and Ave settings are kept when the power is off. Stage records are not kept.
Caution
Do not connect USB-C and DC12V at the same time.

CHAPTER 05Trips (Trip A / Trip B)

Resetting the trips

On the Trip A screen, a short press of the upper CLR resets Trip A to 0, and a short press of the lower CLR resets Trip B. Trip B can also be reset on the clock, speed and final time screens and during a stage.

Setting any distance and measuring from it

  1. On the Trip A screen, long press the upper CLR to enter Trip A.
  2. Upper CLR adds 1 to the blinking digit, lower CLR subtracts 1. The blue button moves to the next digit.
  3. Long press the upper CLR to confirm.
  4. For Trip B, long press the lower CLR on the Trip A screen. Confirm with a long press of the lower CLR as well.

CHAPTER 06Distance calibration

When the displayed distance does not match the road book, adjust it with the calibration factor.

Example: a section of 10.00 km in the road book shows 10.20 km on Trip A.

  1. Long press the red button to enter calibration (C).
  2. Upper CLR adds 1 to the blinking digit, lower CLR subtracts 1. The blue button moves to the next digit.
  3. Set 10.20 ÷ 10.00 = 1.0200.
  4. A short press of the red button saves and returns to the clock. Trip A now shows 10.00 km.
How to calculate
Calibration factor = ARM displayed distance ÷ road book distance

A long press of the red button during calibration returns the factor to 1.0000. The factor also applies to the speed display.

CHAPTER 07Speed and maximum speed

The speed screen shows “S” followed by the calibrated speed. A short press of the upper CLR shows the maximum speed (P); press again to return. The maximum speed is recorded during stage timing and resets to 0 when a stage starts.

CHAPTER 08Speed alert

  1. A short press of the 60 button opens the speed alert setting (A).
  2. Upper CLR adds 10, lower CLR subtracts 10 (10–90 km/h).
  3. A short press of the 60 button turns the alert ON and returns to the previous screen.
  4. To turn it OFF, press the 60 button briefly again.
SpeedRed LED / “A” on the driver display (and the speed on the 2-row version)

A long press of the 60 button also opens the setting (leaving with a long press does not turn the alert ON).

CHAPTER 09Stage timing and stage records

Stage timing

  1. A short press of the red button starts a countdown to 00 s of the next minute. Trip A and Trip B are reset to 0 automatically.
  2. If the car starts moving during the countdown (5 km/h or more), the stage starts at that moment. If it does not move, the stage starts at 00 s. You can also press the red button during the countdown to start immediately.
  3. For 5 seconds after the start the time is shown, then the upper display shows Trip A. The blinking “SS” on the left shows that timing is running.
  4. Every 1 km, the passing time blinks for 3 seconds.
  5. Press the red button at the finish. The time blinks and Trip B returns to 0.
  6. Press the red button again to end the stage. The clock screen shows the finish time blinking for 2 minutes (the upper CLR clears it at once).

The blue button cancels during the countdown. The red button on the remote and the external switch (yellow wire) do the same as the red button.

Stage records

Switch the upper display to “stage records” to view the recorded times. The lower display shows the record number (SS01–SS30). Upper CLR shows the next record, lower CLR the previous one. Up to 30 records are kept; they are lost when the power is off.

CHAPTER 10Ave and final time

When an Ave (target average speed) is set, the final time shows the difference from driving at the Ave, based on the distance and elapsed time since the stage start. When the Ave is OFF, “OFF” is shown.

This function gives a guide to the time remaining until your TC-in when you arrive at the next time control (TC). It helps you check how much time you have in hand.

How to set

  1. On the final time screen, a short press of the upper CLR opens the Ave setting.
  2. Set the Ave on the lower display with the upper CLR: OFF → 30 → 40 … The blue button moves to the next digit.
  3. The upper display shows the start time. It is set automatically to the start time when a stage starts.
  4. Long press the upper CLR to save (the distance so far is kept). Saving with the red button restarts the distance from that point.
Blue LED warning
The blue LED (above the white LED) shows the final time margin: it blinks fast when the margin is under 5 minutes (including when behind), slowly from 5 to 10 minutes, and is off otherwise. It works once an Ave is set and a stage has been started (off while the stage screen is shown).

CHAPTER 11Settings (time offset, brightness, driver display)

Long press the blue button to enter the settings; each short press of the blue button moves to the next setting. The last press returns to the previous screen.

OrderDisplaySettingOperation
1UtcTime offset from UTC (−12 to +12), e.g. UK 0, France +1 (winter), Japan +9.Upper CLR: +1 Lower CLR: −1
2LEdBrightness (3 levels, 0 to 2).Upper CLR: brighter Lower CLR: dimmer
3ddDriver display distance format. “0.01” is normal (12.34 km); “10” enlarges distances under 10 km to 1 m (1.230 km).Upper / lower CLR: switch

The settings are kept when the power is off.

About the driver display distance format

The “10” mode is mainly for recce: it lets the driver judge the length of straights and corners in 10 m steps. Seeing 10 m, 20 m … makes it easier to write clear pace notes.

There are two connectors on the rear: the left one is for the driver display, the right one for the main harness.

External switches

WireFunction
White (Reset trip)Trip B reset only. A short press resets Trip B on the normal screens and the stage screen (not while setting). Useful for adding a foot switch.

Wiring
Connect one side of a momentary switch (ON only while pressed) to the white or yellow wire and the other side to the vehicle GND. If the vehicle GND is not available, use one of the two GND (black) wires on the right of the connector.
