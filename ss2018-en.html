CHAPTER 01Safety precautions

The ARM Twin Trip SS2018 is a tripmeter designed specifically for stage rallies. Based on the experience of ARM products and many co-drivers, it focuses on the functions needed for stage rallies, combining simple operation with an affordable price.

Power supply
This product is for 12 V DC vehicles only. It cannot be installed in vehicles with other voltages (e.g. 24 V).
Fuse
Always fit a fuse between the vehicle power supply and the unit.
Mounting location
Do not install the unit where it obstructs driving or where it does not comply with regulations, and fix it securely. Do not install it where it can get hot or wet.
Wiring
Bundle the unit’s wiring with tape or cable ties and fix it securely to prevent short circuits or broken wires.
Disassembly and modification
This unit is a precision instrument. Disassembly or modification will stop it from working correctly.

Wiring

Connect GND to a point that makes solid contact with the vehicle body. If the point is painted, remove the paint first to avoid a poor contact.

Use momentary switches (ON only while pressed) and connect the two wires of the same color (green or white) to the two terminals of the switch. External switches are sold separately.

CHAPTER 04Power and switching the display

Power

The slide switch on the right side is the power switch. Slide it down for ON; the unit starts and shows the clock on the upper display. Slide it up for OFF.

Switching the upper display

Each short press of the FUNC button switches the upper display clock → sub trip → stage records → clock … The lower display shows the main trip.

ScreenUpper displayExample
ClockCurrent time (h.mm.ss)
Sub tripA distance separate from the main trip (1 m or 10 m steps)
Stage recordsStage time. The lower display shows the stage number
About the clock
The clock keeps running when the power is off, but it may drift by about ±1 second per day. Set the time regularly (chapter 8).
What is kept
Calibration, brightness, pulses and the sub trip unit are kept when the power is off. Stage records are lost when the power is off.

CHAPTER 06Distance calibration

When the displayed distance does not match the road book, adjust it with the calibration factor. The factor is kept when the power is off.

How to calculate
Calibration factor = ARM displayed distance ÷ road book distance

Reset the factor to 1.0000 before measuring the distance you use to calculate it.

Setting the factor

  1. Long press the STAGE button. The factor (C1.0000) appears on the upper display and the digit you can change blinks.
  2. Change the value with the UP / DOWN buttons and move to the next digit with a short press of the FUNC button (0.0001–2.9999).
  3. Press the STAGE button briefly to finish.

Resetting the factor to 1.0000

  1. Long press the STAGE button to show the factor.
  2. Long press the STAGE button again. The factor returns to 1.0000.

CHAPTER 07Stage timing

A stopwatch dedicated to stage rallies. It records the times of 30 stages (they are lost when the power is off).

  1. Stop at the start line and press the STAGE button briefly.
  2. A countdown to 00 s of the next minute starts.
  3. The stopwatch starts when the countdown reaches 0. Even during the countdown, it starts automatically as soon as the car starts moving.
  4. Five seconds after the start, the upper display switches automatically to the sub trip (distance from the start).
  5. Press the STAGE button briefly as you cross the finish line. The stage time is held.
  6. After checking it, press the STAGE or UP button briefly to show the time you crossed the finish line.
  7. Press the STAGE button again to return to the previous screen, or the UP button to return to the clock. Stage timing ends.

CHAPTER 08Settings (clock, brightness, pulses, unit)

Setting the clock

  1. Switch the upper display to the clock and long press the UP button.
  2. Change the blinking value with the UP / DOWN buttons; the FUNC button moves to the next digit.
  3. When the reference time reaches the time you set, press the STAGE button. The clock is set.

Brightness, pulses and sub trip unit

Long press the FUNC button to enter the settings; each short press of the FUNC button moves to the next setting. Pressing FUNC on the last setting returns to the previous screen.

OrderUpper displaySettingOperation
1LEdBrightness (3 levels, 0 to 2)UP: brighter DOWN: dimmer

The settings are kept when the power is off.

CHAPTER 11Warranty and after-sales service

  1. The product comes with a warranty card. The warranty becomes valid when the manufacturer enters the shipping date. Fill in your details and keep the card in a safe place. Warranty cards cannot be reissued.
  2. The warranty period is 6 months from the shipping date on the warranty card.
  3. Within the warranty period, repairs are free of charge under the warranty terms. Repairs not covered by the terms are charged, even within the warranty period.
  4. After the warranty period, the product is repaired for a fee if it can be repaired.
  5. Shipping costs from the customer to the manufacturer and the cost of installation and removal are paid by the customer. The manufacturer pays the return shipping after repair.
  6. If the product turns out to be beyond repair on arrival, the shipping costs are handled in the same way.
  7. The manufacturer does not provide information about the vehicles the product is installed in.
