CHAPTER 01Safety precautions

Power supply
Power the unit from either USB-C (5 V) or DC12V, never both. Supplying power from both at the same time may damage the unit.
Operating while driving
All operation while driving should be done by the co-driver. The driver must concentrate on driving; mount the driver display where it can be read with minimal eye movement.
GPS start-up time
The first time you use the unit, or after it has been switched off for a long time, it may take several minutes before GPS measurement starts. Switch it on well before the event or drive.
GPS reception
Distance and speed are measured by GPS. Reception may not be possible under a roof or in tunnels. After switching on, check GPS reception (GPS LED steady) in an open area before use.
Mounting location
Do not store the unit where water can pour on it directly or where it gets hot (e.g. in direct sunlight on the dashboard).

CHAPTER 03Power and GPS

Using USB-C

  1. Connect 5 V to the USB-C port at the top of the left side. The unit switches on automatically.
  2. While the green GPS LED blinks, the unit is searching for GPS. When it stays on, GPS is received.

Using DC12V

  1. Connect DC12V (red) and GND (black) of the power cable to the 3-pin GX12 connector on the right side.
  2. Slide the switch at the top of the right side down for ON, up for OFF.
  3. The switch is not used when powering from USB-C.
What is kept
TOTAL and PART distances (saved every 10 m), calibration, brightness, time offset and the ±10 m target are kept when the power is off. Up to the last 10 m driven before switching off may not be restored. The stage time is not kept.
Caution
Do not connect USB-C and DC12V at the same time.

Switch the power on while holding the red button to show the firmware version for 2 seconds.

CHAPTER 05TOTAL and PART

Reset

A short press of the TOTAL button resets TOTAL to 0, and a short press of the PART button resets PART. TOTAL is reset even while the upper display shows the clock or speed.

Setting any distance to measure from

  1. Long press the TOTAL button to enter TOTAL (for PART, long press the PART button).
  2. The TOTAL button adds 1 to the blinking digit, the PART button subtracts 1. The right yellow button moves to the next digit, the left one to the previous digit.
  3. Long press the same button again (TOTAL button for TOTAL) to confirm.

CHAPTER 08Distance calibration

When the displayed distance does not match the road book, adjust it with the calibration factor.

Example: a section of 10.00 km in the road book shows 10.20 km on TOTAL.

  1. Long press the red button to enter calibration. The upper display shows “CALIb”, the lower display the factor.
  2. The TOTAL button adds 1 to the blinking digit, the PART button subtracts 1. The right yellow button moves to the next digit, the left one to the previous digit.
  3. Set 10.20 ÷ 10.00 = 1.0200. Each change is saved immediately.
  4. Long press the red button again to return.
How to calculate
Calibration factor = ARM displayed distance ÷ road book distance

The factor also applies to the speed display.

CHAPTER 09Stage timing

  1. Switch the upper display to the stage screen (“------”) with the blue button.
  2. A short press of the red button starts a countdown to 00 s of the next minute. TOTAL and PART are reset to 0.
  3. At 00 s the stage starts automatically. Press the red button during the countdown to start immediately, or the blue button to cancel.
  4. While running, the elapsed time (h.mm.ss) is shown with blinking separators.
  5. Press the red button at the finish. The time blinks and PART returns to 0.
  6. Press the red button again to return to standby (“------”).

The stage time is lost when the power is off.
